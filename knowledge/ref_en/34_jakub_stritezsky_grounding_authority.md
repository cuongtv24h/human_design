He naturally shares the system, especially in his home regions, the Czech and Slovak Republics. His years of apprenticeship with Genoa Blevin, the Director of Human Design America, have shaped Jakob’s unique understanding and approach. His work reflects his incarnation cross, empowering others to achieve relational and spiritual abundance together. In this episode, Jakob shares his journey from meditation practices to discovering Human Design as a source of grounding and professional purpose. He offers valuable insights into Authority, describing it as an island of stability in the ocean of life, and emphasizes recognizing one’s personal experience of genuine inner guidance. During the conversation he shares his passion for teaching and making the system relevant to everyday life. Emphasizing that transformation through Human Design requires courage, patience, and surrender.
